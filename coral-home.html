Unusual by design

A house with
no sharp corners.

Round walls. Stone paths. Little arches that lead somewhere new. Every room here is different, and it feels like walking through a small fairytale labyrinth.

The style is called bionic design. It copies shapes from nature, so there are almost no straight lines. The house is built from recycled materials. Solar panels give the power. Water goes through eco-filters.
