Program 1 · Discovery

Bali, with real meaning.

  • 1 coral restoration dive day a week. 2 hands-on scuba dives: coral planting, reef care, plus a Coral Talk.
  • 6 days that are yours. Cafés, waterfalls, freediving, your own projects.
  • No marine biology needed. You do need an Open Water scuba certificate. No license yet? No problem: we connect you with a partner dive school, and you get your PADI or SSI Open Water in Bali before the program (~$320–350).
What’s included
  • 2 hands-on coral restoration scuba dives every week
  • Coral planting, structure care and guided observation
  • Coral Talks and marine conservation sessions
  • Stay at Coral Home (shared room; more comfort at extra cost)
  • 1 lunch per week on dive day
  • Airport pickup and drop-off
  • Mask, fins, snorkeling gear and SUP
  • Coral Sense team t-shirt
  • Help with local logistics

Program 2 · Research

1 month that feeds your studies and your CV.

  • 4 coral restoration scuba dives a week in 2 different reef gardens.
  • Live experiments: substrate, depth, species competition.
  • Field skills: photo protocols, monitoring, AI-assisted analysis.
How a month unfolds
Week 1

Foundations

Meet the team, the reef and the structures. Join planting and reef care.

Week 2

Care & monitoring

Structure maintenance, documenting changes, photo protocols.

Week 3

Data & AI

Organize observations and learn how AI tools help with field data.

Week 4

Your own voice

More independent dives. When ready, short briefings for guests.

What’s included
  • 4 hands-on coral restoration scuba dives every week
  • Full dive equipment
  • Training: coral restoration, conservation, research thinking, AI workflows
  • Both gardens: Amed + Padang Bai, with transfer
  • Stay at Coral Home (shared room; more comfort at extra cost)
  • 2 lunches per week on dive days
  • Airport pickup and drop-off · Snorkel gear, SUP
  • Team t-shirt · Certificate of Completion
Requirements
  • Background in marine biology, conservation or a related field
  • Open Water (can be done in Bali)
  • Personal laptop · Able to ride a scooter
