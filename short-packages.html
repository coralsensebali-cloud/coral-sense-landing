A full day, 9:00 to 19:00

Every day on the reef is a little different.

The reef decides what needs doing today. Sometimes it's planting, sometimes rescuing broken corals, sometimes caring for the structures.

  • Morning
    Coral TalkCoral science in simple words. Why reefs matter and what we do for them.
  • Dive 1
    Into the gardenRescue damaged corals that would die without help, and plant them with your own hands.
  • Midday
    Lunch and restGood food, a break, time to talk about what you saw.
  • Dive 2
    Deeper into restorationResearch, coral care, reef building, learning to identify coral species. With the half-day option, you skip this one.
  • Evening
    Sunset1 of the best volcano views on the island. You get photos and your personal Ocean Impact Certificate.

Where you're going

Amed.
The real Bali.

No tourist crowds. Instead, one of the best underwater worlds in Bali and wild, beautiful nature. Ocean lovers come here on purpose.

  • Under water: a shipwreck, underwater statues, coral gardens, turtles. Sometimes dolphins, and now and then whales pass by.
  • Dozens of snorkel spots right from the shore.
  • The best place to learn scuba diving and freediving.
  • Our coral gardens are here too.

You don’t need to figure anything out alone. We’ll connect you with the best budget-friendly dive and freedive centres, guides and drivers. With us you have a family in Bali.
