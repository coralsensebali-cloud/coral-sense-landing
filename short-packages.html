Short Experience

Just visiting Bali for a couple of days?

Two days at our coral restoration garden in Amed. Four dives, real hands-on work — coral planting, reef care, maintenance. No long-term commitment, no tourist dive format.

Ask About Dates
2 Days / 4 Dives

Two full dive days at our Amed garden.

Each day follows the same rhythm as a regular program day — morning briefing, two dives, lunch, debrief. You join the team and do what the team does.

Day 1

Coral Restoration — Amed Garden

First day in the water. Morning coral talk, two guided dives at our Amed site. Coral planting, maintenance of structures, reef care. Lunch included. By the end of day one you already know how the site works.

Day 2

Deeper Into the Reef

Second day builds on the first. Two more dives — different tasks, more confidence underwater, more time with the reef. You finish with an Ocean Impact Certificate and a clear sense of what coral restoration actually looks like in practice.

What You'll Do
  • Coral maintenance and care of underwater structures
  • Rescue of broken coral fragments
  • Coral fragmentation and preparation
  • Coral planting on restoration sites
  • Collecting basic research data
What's Included
  • 4 scuba dives at Amed coral garden
  • Full dive equipment
  • Coral restoration materials
  • Coral Conservation Training
  • Lunch on both dive days
  • Coral Sense t-shirt
  • Ocean Impact Certificate
